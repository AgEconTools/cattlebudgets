Retained ownership budget

Keep your weaned calves, send them to a feedlot, and sell them as fed cattle. The calves are charged at what they would have netted at a Mississippi auction at weaning, so the net return is what retaining earns over selling at weaning. The sale price starts from live cattle futures for the sale month plus the usual basis.

Freezer beef budget

Finish your own cattle and sell the beef by the whole, half or quarter, priced per pound of hanging weight. Your own calves are charged at what they would bring at auction, so the net return is what finishing and selling direct earns over selling them at the barn.
